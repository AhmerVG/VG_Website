One chain, two stages.

How VGreen operates: which party performs which function, who pays whom, and what is disclosed to both sides. A cluster starts with VGreen holding both contracts on the same terms, one with the buyer and one with the farmer. As it becomes a hub, the hub contracts with the market in its own name and VGreen moves alongside it as management, advisory and services.
