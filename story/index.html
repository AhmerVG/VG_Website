The origins of the VGreen model.

Vital Green (Private) Limited was incorporated in 2019 after a soft pilot from 2018, and the first hub was established in 2020. Today the company works with 10,200 farmers across four hubs, eighteen clusters and fifteen districts, and has served more than 85,000 cumulative acres since 2018. Founding members: Ahmed Umair and Tahir Abbas. Shareholders: Vital Agri Nutrients and Descon Holdings Limited (Agri Ventures).
